# Privacy Policy for FrostBackup

**Last Updated: October 2026**

FrostBackup is a Free and Open Source (GPL-3.0) Android application committed to total user privacy and data sovereignty.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

* **Zero Telemetry:** FrostBackup does not collect, log, track, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers.
* **On-Device Processing:** All backup archives, database dumps, and cryptographic operations are executed locally on your Android device.

---

## 2. Google Drive Data & Permissions

When you connect Google Drive to FrostBackup:

* **Scope (`drive.appdata`):** The application only requests access to its own private Application Data folder (`appDataFolder`).
* **Isolation:** FrostBackup cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your personal files, photos, sheets, or documents stored on Google Drive.
* **Zero-Knowledge Encryption:** If you enable encryption, backup files are encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM before uploading to Google Drive. Neither Google nor third parties can decrypt your data without your master password.
* **No Third-Party Transmission:** Google Drive credentials and authentication tokens are stored strictly on your local device and are never transmitted to any third-party server or developer backend.
