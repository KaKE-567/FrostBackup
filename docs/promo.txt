Title:
Made an open source root backup app that keeps your logins alive after restoring (FrostBackup)

Body:
Hey guys,

Like a lot of you, I've had issues with Titanium Backup and other backup tools logging me out of apps like Discord, WhatsApp, Twitter, and games every time I wipe my phone or switch ROMs on Android 14/15/16/17.

The main reason this happens on modern Android is because apps check their SSAID (Android ID) and Keystore keys. When you reinstall or restore normally, the OS assigns a new UID and a new Android ID, which invalidates your session on the server.

I made FrostBackup (free & GPLv3) to solve that:
- It saves and restores each app's SSAID before the app even starts.
- Migrates Keystore keys and remaps them to the new Linux UID assigned by Android.
- Fixes SELinux context and permissions automatically so nothing crashes.
- Has a Calls & SMS backup tab with deduplication so you don't get duplicate texts when restoring.
- Optional local AES-256 encryption.
- Direct Google Drive sync so you don't need a PC.
- Material 3 dark/AMOLED UI with a Pixel-style squiggly slider.

Works with Magisk, KernelSU, and APatch (Android 8.0 to 17+).

Source code: https://github.com/KaKE-567/FrostBackup
APK download: https://github.com/KaKE-567/FrostBackup/releases/latest
Docs/Site: https://kake-567.github.io/FrostBackup/

Let me know if you run into any bugs or have feature ideas, would love to hear your feedback!
