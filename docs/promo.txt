[DEV] FrostBackup — Free & Open Source Root Backup app that preserves login sessions, SSAID & Keystore tokens across ROM flashes

[BODY]
Hey everyone! 👋

I got tired of restoring app data on new ROMs only to find myself logged out of Discord, Twitter, WhatsApp, and games because of Android's modern security architecture (SSAID mismatch & lost Keystore tokens).

So I built **FrostBackup** — a 100% Free and Open Source (GPL-3.0) root backup solution built with Jetpack Compose & Material 3.

### ⚡ What Makes It Different:
* **True Login Session Retention:** Automatically migrates per-app SSAIDs (settings_ssaid.xml) and re-binds Keystore cryptographic keys (/data/misc/keystore/user_0/) to the newly assigned Linux UIDs upon restore.
* **Calls & SMS Tab:** Back up call logs and SMS messages via direct SQLite (mmssms.db, calllog.db) with smart deduplication on restore.
* **Pixel Squiggly Slider UI:** Features Google Pixel-style undulating wavy progress bars, Dynamic Monet theming, and pure black AMOLED mode.
* **Live Google Drive Cloud Sync:** Direct REST v3 upload with real-time streaming progress (e.g. 45.2 / 106.0 MB).
* **Military-Grade Encryption:** Local AES-256-GCM authenticated cipher with PBKDF2 (65,536 rounds).
* **Root Support:** Works out-of-the-box on Magisk, KernelSU, and APatch (Android 8.0 to 15+).

[B][SIZE=4]⚡ The Problem FrostBackup Solves:[/SIZE][/B]
Legacy backup apps (Titanium Backup, basic data copiers) often leave you logged out of Discord, WhatsApp, Twitter, Steam, and games after restoring.

On modern Android (Android 8 to 15+), app sessions depend on hardware signatures and cryptographic keys. FrostBackup addresses this directly:
1. [B]SSAID Preservation:[/B] Captures and restores per-app Android IDs ([ICODE]settings_ssaid.xml[/ICODE]) before first launch so apps don't detect a device change.
2. [B]Keystore Token Migration:[/B] Backs up system-level Keystore cryptographic keys and dynamically remaps them to newly assigned Linux UIDs upon restore.
3. [B]SELinux & UID Auto-Healing:[/B] Automatically executes recursive [ICODE]restorecon -R[/ICODE] and [ICODE]chown -R[/ICODE] so restored apps never crash with permission denials.

---

[B][SIZE=4]🌟 Key Features:[/SIZE][/B]
• [B]Universal Root Support:[/B] Seamlessly works with [B]Magisk[/B], [B]KernelSU[/B], and [B]APatch[/B].
• [B]Dedicated Calls & SMS Tab:[/B] Dual-engine (Root SQLite [ICODE]mmssms.db[/ICODE] + ContentProvider) with smart deduplicated restore.
• [B]Zero-Knowledge AES-256-GCM Encryption:[/B] PBKDF2 with 65,536 iterations.
• [B]Direct Google Drive Sync:[/B] Resumable chunked uploads directly into your personal Drive with live MB/percent progress tracking.
• [B]Pixel Squiggly UI & AMOLED Mode:[/B] Material 3 with animated squiggly progress waves, 15 preset accents, Dynamic Monet, and pure black #000000 dark mode.
